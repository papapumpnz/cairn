# CAIRN experiment ladder

The ladder is designed to test the central architecture before expensive full-suite runs. Thresholds are hypotheses to pre-register before implementation of each measured stage. A failed gate is recorded; it is not weakened retroactively. Section order is reporting order, not permission to defer a learning loop: task learning, continual learning, and interactive causal learning begin as soon as their foundation contracts exist.

## 0. Dataset policy

### Development partitions

- Synthetic mechanism suites authored independently from the solver implementation.
- ARC-AGI-1 training split.
- ARC-AGI-2 training split.
- Public ARC-AGI-3 development environments under the benchmark's permitted rules.

### Quarantined partitions

- ARC-AGI-1 evaluation.
- ARC-AGI-2 public evaluation after the development protocol is frozen.
- ARC-AGI-2 semi-private and private evaluation.
- ARC-AGI-3 hidden environments.

No persistent module may be promoted from a quarantined task.

Generated grid, gridworld, and vector-graphics suites each have frozen generator
seeds, hidden structural combinations, and adapter-blind core metrics. ARC is one
adapter evaluation and cannot be the source of a general-core requirement.

## 1. Measurement law

Every experiment directory contains:

```text
hypothesis.md
config.toml
run_log.md
benchmark_result.json
per_task_results.jsonl or per_case_results.jsonl
evidence.md
next_experiment_suggestion.md
```

Before a measured run:

- hypothesis and thresholds committed;
- suite frozen;
- seeds frozen;
- code commit frozen;
- baseline frozen;
- machine idle policy declared;
- expected failure taxonomy declared.

After a measured run:

- run once unless repeats were pre-registered;
- all negatives retained;
- no task-shaped patch before reporting;
- all certificates replayed by the independent checker.

## 2. General-learning continuation gates

These gates are the claim boundary for CAIRN as a general learner. ARC scores do
not substitute for any gate.

### GLL-1 — omitted-concept invention

Across multiple unrelated generated families, remove a required relation or
constructor from the initial vocabulary. CAIRN must invent an executable
definition and solve held-out changes in geometry, count, categorical values,
and position. Passing one fixed family is insufficient.

### GLL-2 — cross-task improvement

On a frozen structurally related test set at the same CPU budget, compare memory
enabled with memory disabled. Memory must reduce median search by at least 50%,
reduce demonstrations required, preserve exact accuracy, and rebind transferred
definitions across changed values, sizes, counts, and layouts. Unrelated tasks
must not regress.

### GLL-3 — skill composition

Learn two mechanisms independently, then solve a held-out task requiring their
composition without a newly coded composite operator. The certificate must show
both learned mechanisms rebound and composed through ordinary theory machinery.

### GLL-4 — unknown-world learning

In procedurally generated environments with unseen transition laws, predict
action outcomes, infer an executable goal, plan, improve action efficiency over
episodes, and transfer a learned option to changed layouts.

### GLL-5 — cross-domain representation

Use the same evidence graph, theory representation, ontology invention,
verifier, memory promotion, and planning machinery on grid transformations,
interactive grid worlds, and vector graphics or diagram manipulation. Frontends
may differ; cognition code and semantic contracts may not.

### GLL-6 — ARC quarantine

Every proposed core mechanism has an environment-neutral definition, an
independent synthetic family, variation across claimed generalization axes, a
non-ARC example where applicable, and an ablation. No core change is admitted
solely because it solves an ARC task.

## 3. Phase F: foundations

### F0 — CPU and transformer exclusion

Question: can prohibited execution paths be detected mechanically?

Gate:

- checker catches planted CUDA, ROCm, OpenCL, transformer-library, remote-model, and task-ID violations;
- runtime attestation detects a planted loaded accelerator library;
- clean workspace passes.

### F1 — finite-field and relation kernels

Question: are low-level semantics correct and fast?

Gate:

- exhaustive equivalence to naive reference on all grids up to 5 by 5 for defined kernels;
- at least 100,000 randomized cases up to 30 by 30;
- at least 20 million benchmarked primitive operations per core per second on baseline hardware;
- no undefined behavior under sanitizer and Miri-compatible subsets.

### F2 — RKL totality and proof replay

Question: can arbitrary typed bounded programs execute safely and replay identically?

Gate:

- type soundness property tests;
- random typed program fuzzing with no panic;
- bound checker rejects planted non-total definitions;
- independent verifier agrees with optimized executor on at least one million random programs over small grids.

## 4. Phase E: event induction

### E0 — correspondence family suite

Independent generator emits preserve, move, transform, recolor, copy, delete, split, merge, create, aggregate, and distribute transitions. Solver receives raw grids only.

Gate:

- correct event kind present in top five hypotheses for at least 98% of single-event cases;
- exact cover present in top eight for at least 95% of two-event cases;
- false exact covers below 1% on adversarial distractor cases;
- median below 20 ms per pair.

### E1 — event-cover versus forward enumeration

Compare event-derived sketches with a size-matched forward RKL enumerator under identical CPU time.

Gate:

- event method solves at least twice as many multi-event held-out cases;
- at least 5 times fewer executed complete programs per solve;
- no accuracy regression on single-event cases.

Failure falsifies H1.

### E2 — joint perception ablation

Compare:

```text
A: fixed top-ranked segmentation
B: precomputed parse lattice
C: demand-driven joint entity and theory induction
```

Suite includes multicolor objects, deceptive backgrounds, panels, nested regions, and motifs.

Gate:

- C improves exact pair coverage by at least 10 percentage points over A;
- C improves at least 5 points over B at equal CPU budget;
- median entity materialization remains below 100 ms.

Failure falsifies H2 or its implementation.

## 5. Phase T: theory formation

### T0 — contextual guard induction

Generate tasks with identical object appearance but different relational roles. Include hard negatives.

Gate:

- at least 95% held-out selector accuracy;
- zero accepted theory that changes a hard negative on demonstrations;
- literal-color and literal-position ablations show relational clauses are causally used.

### T1 — variable-arity construction

Suites vary object counts, sizes, positions, and transforms while preserving mechanism.

Gate:

- at least 95% held-out exact outputs;
- count range in held-out exceeds training range;
- no fixed-slot fallback accepted;
- output provenance accounts for every created entity.

### T2 — multi-rule composition

Suites contain independent, dependent, conflicting, and fixpoint rules.

Gate:

- at least 90% held-out exact output overall;
- at least 85% on interacting-rule cases;
- schedule recovered up to semantic equivalence in at least 90% of solved cases;
- one-rule ablation loses at least 30 points, confirming the suite is nontrivial.

### T3 — symbolic interpretation

Symbols control colors, directions, transforms, selectors, constructors, and schedule branches. Visual appearance is permuted independently of semantics across task families.

Gate:

- at least 90% held-out exact output;
- at least 80% on operator-valued meanings;
- no more than 2% false acceptance on unrelated symbols;
- symbol-map removal causes the expected collapse.

### T4 — ontology invention

The base search vocabulary deliberately omits derived relations and constructors needed by the suite, while RKL remains capable of defining them.

Gate:

- invention solves at least 70% of previously unrealizable cases;
- no task-specific primitive names or IDs;
- invented definitions transfer to held-out instances with changed counts and geometry;
- exact negative suite false positives below 2%;
- median invention overhead below 10 s.

Failure falsifies H4.

### T5 — learning from failures ablation

Compare full CEGIS constraints against candidate rejection without learned constraints.

Gate:

- at least 3 times fewer candidate executions on median;
- at least 2 times lower p95 solve time;
- same or higher exact solve rate.

Failure weakens H5.

## 6. Phase A: ambiguity and generalization

### A0 — leave-one-demonstration-out prediction

On training-only tasks with at least three demonstrations, measure whether ranking by leave-one-out improves held-out demonstration prediction versus MDL alone.

Gate:

- at least 8 percentage-point improvement on ambiguous tasks;
- no more than 2-point regression overall.

### A1 — pass@2 diversity

Synthetic tasks are generated from two plausible rule families that agree on demonstrations and differ on test inputs, with a known prior distribution.

Gate:

- pass@2 exceeds best single-mode by at least 10 points;
- second attempt duplicates first on fewer than 1% of ambiguous cases;
- diversity does not reduce pass@1.

### A2 — memory promotion

Train mechanism modules on broad and narrow variation regimes.

Gate:

- broad modules transfer at least 90%;
- narrow modules explicitly retain uncertainty and do not overclaim unsupported dimensions;
- disabling memory preserves correctness but increases median search by at least 2 times;
- memory reduces demonstrations required on structurally related held-out tasks;
- unrelated held-out tasks show no exact-accuracy regression;
- memory causes zero unverified accepts.

This is the first GLL-2 measurement, not a late optimization after ARC.

## 7. Phase R1: ARC-AGI-1 adapter evaluation

### R1.0 — frozen 60-task diagnostic

Select 60 ARC-AGI-1 training tasks before engine integration, stratified by:

```text
global transform
object edit
variable-arity construction
crop and canvas
symbol or key
multi-rule
contextual selector
iterative pattern
```

Do not choose only tasks known to fit the architecture.

Initial gate:

- at least 36/60 exact at 30 s;
- at least one solve in every family;
- fewer than half of failures may be `no_pair_event_cover`;
- no family with 0/5 or worse unless diagnosed as a named architectural gap.

The threshold is lower than MOSAIC's failed 50/60 because this suite must be broader and the first run tests integration. A later 50/60 gate follows after one taxonomy-driven general iteration.

### R1.1 — full 400 training sweep

Uniform scheduling, no learned cross-task memory except verified core definitions.

Gate 1:

- at least 120/400 exact pass@2;
- median below 15 s;
- at least 70% of solved tasks pass@1;
- failure taxonomy and ablations committed.

### R1.2 — one general taxonomy-driven iteration

One architectural change may be chosen from the dominant failure class. It must be a general mechanism with a synthetic pre-test.

Gate 2:

- at least 180/400;
- gain at least 30 tasks outside the mechanism's development subset;
- zero task-ID code;
- same config rerun within measured noise.

### R1.3 — mature static engine

After no more than three general iterations:

Target:

- at least 240/400 training;
- p95 below 120 s;
- no more than 20% failures classified as kernel or ontology closure.

Kill criterion:

- two consecutive general iterations add fewer than 10 tasks and do not shift taxonomy.

## 8. Phase R2: ARC-AGI-2 adapter evaluation

ARC-AGI-2 training is used for mechanism development, not public evaluation tuning.

### R2.0 — family diagnostics

Measure separate subsets for symbolic interpretation, composition, and context.

Gate:

- at least 50% on a human-labeled or independently classified 120-task training diagnostic;
- no headline family below 35%;
- symbol and composition ablations materially reduce relevant scores.

### R2.1 — full 1,000-task training sweep

Initial target:

- at least 250/1,000 exact pass@2;
- median below 20 s;
- at least 50 unique solves not attributable to ARC-AGI-1 legacy mechanisms.

### R2.2 — architecture continuation gate

Continue toward evaluation only if:

- at least 350/1,000 after general iterations;
- learning curves are not saturated;
- held-out training folds show less than 5-point degradation;
- no evidence of task-family lookup.

## 9. Gated ARC evaluations

### G1 — ARC-AGI-1 evaluation

One sanctioned run after R1.3.

Report:

- pass@1 and pass@2;
- Wilson confidence intervals;
- CPU cost;
- overlap with SCEM and MOSAIC solves where records permit;
- memory-disabled and swarm-disabled ablations on a predeclared subset or full set if affordable.

No threshold is used to rewrite the architecture. The result is evidence.

### G2 — ARC-AGI-2 public evaluation

One sanctioned run after R2.2.

Initial continuation bars:

- 10% pass@2: architecture produces meaningful signal;
- 20%: continue optimization;
- 35%: strong symbolic CPU system;
- 50%: begin private-evaluation campaign;
- 85%: benchmark pass objective.

These bars do not imply expected scores.

### G3 — semi-private and private

Only after the public protocol, software, and resource budget are frozen. Official evaluation procedures take precedence.

## 10. Phase I: interactive causal learning

Phase I starts after F2 and the generic observation/action contracts; it does
not wait for R1 or R2. Its controlled suites are co-developed with static theory
induction so action-conditioned counterexamples shape the common representation.

### I0 — controlled transition induction

Hidden gridworlds vary action effects, obstacles, keys, switches, and goals.

Gate:

- transition prediction at least 95% after bounded exploration;
- active policy uses at least 30% fewer actions than random probing;
- hidden-state variables correspond to independently testable effects.

### I1 — goal inference

No goal label is given. Environments expose sparse progress or terminal changes.

Gate:

- correct executable goal predicate in top three for at least 90%;
- at least 80% episode completion;
- goal ablation causes expected collapse.

### I2 — option induction

Gate:

- learned options reduce planning-node expansions by at least 5 times on held-out layouts;
- option success at least 90% where initiation condition holds;
- false initiation below 5%.

Add a composition suite in which containment and ordering (or two unrelated
mechanisms of equivalent difficulty) are learned separately and later required
together. Passing this suite is GLL-3.

### I3 — ARC-AGI-3 public development environments

Report completion, action efficiency, theory quality, and failure taxonomy. No environment-specific source code is allowed.

Continuation gate:

- meaningful progress on every development environment;
- complete at least half with one general configuration;
- action count improves across repeated levels as theories and options are learned online.

## 11. Cross-domain experiment

### D0 — shared-core three-domain run

Run frozen grid-transformation, interactive-gridworld, and vector-graphics
families through their adapters and one cognition runtime.

Gate:

- all domains emit the same generic evidence, theory, verification, memory, and planning artifact schemas;
- omitted-concept invention succeeds in at least two unrelated families per domain;
- one promoted mechanism transfers across two adapters where its semantics apply;
- removing any adapter leaves core crates byte-for-byte unchanged;
- domain-specific labels or task identifiers are absent from core semantic hashes.

Passing D0 is GLL-5. A domain-specific frontend success without common reasoning
does not count.

## 12. Swarm experiments

### S0 — local speed scaling

Compare 1, 2, 4, and 8 workers.

Gate:

- at least 1.7 times at 2 workers;
- at least 3.0 times at 4 workers;
- at least 4.5 times at 8 workers on search-heavy tasks;
- deterministic semantic results across worker counts.

### S1 — capability scaling

Compare homogeneous theory-solver workers with the full heterogeneous portfolio at equal CPU time.

Gate:

- heterogeneous swarm solves at least 10% more tasks on the diagnostic suite;
- unique gains attributable to at least three non-solver roles;
- falsifier or inventor artifacts demonstrably change successful theories.

### S2 — distributed integrity

Gate:

- forged, corrupted, version-mismatched, and resource-misreported artifacts rejected;
- node loss recovers without semantic corruption;
- network overhead below 15% on a four-node task-sharded workload.

Every major benchmark report includes, where applicable:

## 14. Final pass and claim definition

General-learning pass:

```text
GLL-1 through GLL-6 all pass on frozen evidence packs
all three learning loops show positive held-out results
the same cognition runtime operates across three required domains
every accepted prediction or plan remains verifier-controlled
```

Until then, CAIRN is described as a hypothesis for a learning architecture. A
successful component may be described precisely; the system may not be called a
generally learning AI.

ARC adapter pass:

```text
at least 85% exact pass@2 on a clean ARC-AGI-2 private evaluation
CPU-only
transformer-free
reproducible resource report
open, replayable method under the applicable competition rules
```

Interactive benchmark pass:

```text
complete the official environment set
with action efficiency at or near the benchmark's human baseline
without environment-specific harness logic
using the same general-purpose CAIRN API and memory system
```

## C0 — verified technical-chat response

Given “Give me a basic Python function”, the system must compile an explicit
intent, choose a documented ambiguity policy, construct a typed Python AST,
execute at least two examples, and render source only after verification. An
unknown request must clarify rather than fabricate a task. No external model is
available during the test.

## D0 — basic symbolic distillation

A frozen offline teacher packet provides a paraphrase mapping and executable
scalar examples. Required result: closed-schema parsing, typed compilation,
dual-executor verification, permanent origin, two distinct peer verifiers,
changed-context transfer evidence, swarm acceptance, and successful use after
the raw packet and teacher are removed. Author self-verification must fail.

## D1 — consolidated teacher independence

Across at least three varied domains, compare raw examples with consolidated
mechanisms. Teacher access is removed. Required evidence: unseen wording,
changed values/entities/cardinalities, one novel composition, incorrect-use
detection, repair, cross-agent transfer, lower search/sample cost, and negative
transfer below 2%.

## D2 — non-ARC knowledge transfer to ARC

Freeze an ARC set before distillation. Compare blank memory with memory learned
only from programming, vector, sequence, and interactive examples. Imported
mechanisms must appear in proof traces, be rebound and replayed on every current
demonstration, reduce candidate executions or improve exact solves, and cause
no false acceptance. Complete ARC pairs, identifiers, and structurally identical
teacher puzzles are forbidden. Run the same ablation with transferred artifacts
removed to establish causal contribution.

## G5-LAYER: frozen relational construction and renderer transfer

Freeze four non-ARC vector-graphics learning examples in two independently
verified cohorts before opening the ARC gate. Vary extents, positions,
categorical values, and point cardinalities. Require the learner to recover the
same set expression, role bindings, foreground binding, and render frame in
both cohorts without a named graphics or grid operation.

Acceptance requires: provisional status after one cohort; persistence after
two disjoint cohorts; exact changed-vector replay; exact transfer through a
finite-field frontend; explicit support geometry; no blank-memory proposal; and
failure under wrong-renderer and changed-expected ablations. Separately require
the same learner to induce intersection/tight-content and ordered difference,
and reject tied roles and capacity exhaustion.

After implementation, tests, documentation, and the ARC harness are committed,
run first-40 and complete-400 ARC training ablations once. Compare to the frozen
18/40 and 36/400 baselines. Do not change the grammar, bindings, or thresholds
in response to the result. Preserve every per-case terminal state and report a
flat or negative result as evidence about applicability.

Measured result: non-ARC G5-LAYER passes, but the ARC contribution gate fails.
All structural memory produces 36/400 exact and zero incorrect; layer-only and
blank memory both produce 17/40. Preserve the disabled failing causal-ablation
test and evidence hashes in the experiment report. Per ADR-0024, stop
ARC-directed growth of this grammar and require the next correspondence learner
to beat blank memory under an otherwise identical frozen evaluation.

## G5-CORRESPONDENCE: entity mapping with construction provenance

Freeze two independently authored vector cohorts before ARC. Vary source IDs,
values, extents, positions, ordering, entity counts, point cardinalities, axis
orientation, and frame. Require one learned mechanism to reconstruct all output
entities and carry complete source entity/point provenance.

Acceptance requires exact tight-reflection and preserved-axis-permutation
families; two-cohort promotion; explicit support; changed-family held-outs;
strict candidate and example reduction under persistent reuse; exact
vector-to-field transfer; zero verifier-accepted wrong-family proposals; and
failure on ambiguity, collision, changed truth, duplicate evidence, and caps.

Only after a committed freeze, run blank versus correspondence-only ARC first
40. Strict exact accuracy improvement with zero incorrect is mandatory. If it
passes, run combined first 40 and full 400. If it fails, preserve evidence and
stop before the complete run; applicability does not satisfy the gate.

Measured result: the source-domain gate passes, but ARC blank and
correspondence-only memory both score 17/40 exact with zero incorrect. The
mandatory strict-gain assertion fails, so full-400 was not run. Preserve the
failure under ADR-0026 and require a richer non-bijective, relation-conditioned
mechanism to restart at the non-ARC source gate.

## G0-PROGRAM: rule structure synthesized from verified traces

Use v1 only to create independently verified non-ARC traces. Synthesize extents
and coordinates through generic RKL invention. The program may contain no
transform enum, named operation, v1 configuration, source ID/value, or modality.

Remove v1 before held-out execution. Require changed held-outs, independent
verifier replay, field transfer, changed-truth rejection, and capability loss
when a learned definition is removed. Preserve the initial two-trace
overgeneralization failure.

This gate does not authorize ARC. Next require equivalent programs from
independent correspondence-discovery cohorts and promotion without v1 traces.

## G2-PROGRAM: autonomous program-library learning

Freeze two disjoint four-example cohorts containing unordered source/output
entity sets and no point correspondences or v1 traces. Require canonical program
agreement, independent receipts, and two-cohort promotion.

On larger held-outs require exact program-only execution, at least 100x fewer
attempts than fresh synthesis, zero new examples, blank-memory failure, field
transfer, and zero accepted wrong-family cases. Reordering must preserve the
definition; missing outputs and caps fail closed.

Measured: eight experiences promote one program; 5,986 candidates become two
attempts; examples fall from four to zero; two held-outs and one field transfer
are exact; negative accepts are zero. ARC remains closed.

## G2-CONDITIONAL: relation-conditioned non-bijective program learning

Freeze relational collections with changing entity counts and unordered
renders. Invent the selector and joint constructor without selected IDs or
point correspondences. Require two verified cohorts, held-out cardinalities
beyond training, zero-example reuse, a field-adapter transfer, blank-memory
failure, and changed-truth rejection.

Measured: 140 fresh candidates become two memory attempts and zero examples;
held-outs 2/2, field transfer 1/1, negative accepts zero. ARC-10 remains 10/10
in both conditions; ARC-20 terminates at the fixed 600-second command cap.

## G2-PATH-EXPAND

Require two-edge path selection where all one-edge features fail on hard
negatives, plus unordered one-to-many construction without paired outputs.
Both must cross two verified cohorts, reduce future candidate/example work,
transfer to higher cardinality, survive order changes, and fail on ablation or
changed truth. Measured path work is 110 to two attempts; expansion work is 522
to one attempt; future examples are zero and negative accepts are zero.

Post-freeze ARC regression: conditional first-ten remains 10/10 in both blank
and memory conditions with zero incorrect; the official frozen first-20 remains
17/20. This is a no-improvement result.

## G2-GROUP-MERGE

Require unordered many-to-one construction over supplied finite groups. The
target must compose aggregates rather than copy one. Require two verified
cohorts, larger held-outs, zero-example reuse, blank-memory failure, and
wrong-family rejection. Measured: 185 fresh candidates become one memory
attempt; six held-out groups are exact and wrong accepts are zero.

## G0-JOINT-GROUPING

Provide multiple equal-cardinality grouping hypotheses and no selected role.
At least one hard alternative must be functionally inconsistent with unordered
outputs. Require one cross-episode executable grouping/theory, exact held-out
rebinding, and missing-role failure. Measured: two candidates, four held-out
groups exact, distractor exact theories zero.

## G2-RELATION-BOUND-GROUPING

Independently learn the same grouping/aggregate composition in two disjoint
non-ARC cohorts. Require identity agreement, independent raw-RKL replay, a
consolidator-issued promotion certificate, zero-example held-out reuse, blank
memory failure, duplicate-evidence rejection, and certificate failure after a
relation change.

Measured: two receipts promote one composition; fresh candidate work is 187
versus one proposal attempt, examples are four versus zero, and nine changed
held-out groups replay exactly. Blank and changed-relation acceptances are zero.
Raw relation generation and durable restart are required next.

## G0/G2-RAW-RELATION-GROUPING

Supply opaque entities and at least two unselected relation-edge sets; supply no
candidate partitions. Require deterministic complete component generation,
