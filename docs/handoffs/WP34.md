## Known limits

- Initial entity/relation evidence remains generator-authored rather than
  learned from raw sensors.
- Relational selectors are bounded two-round fingerprints, not arbitrary path
  predicates or recursive learned definitions, and remain task-local.
- Unknown-world laws are deterministic and exact; active learning supports only
  one-step distinguishing continuations in resettable episodes.
- Persistent latent transfer covers five finite structures but remains exact,
  additive, low-dimensional, and deterministic. Sequential experiment planning
  operates over supplied executable theory hypotheses and is not yet coupled to
  latent-theory synthesis or stochastic belief.
- Internal mechanism mining remains contiguous-window proof-schema learning,
  not arbitrary RKL world-law induction.
- Five small synthetic world structures cannot establish broad general
  intelligence.
- ARC remains 36/400; this milestone adds zero ARC solves.
