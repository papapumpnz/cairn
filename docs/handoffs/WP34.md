## Known limits

- Initial entity/relation evidence remains generator-authored rather than
  learned from raw sensors.
- Relational selectors are bounded two-round fingerprints, not arbitrary path
  predicates or recursive learned definitions, and remain task-local.
- Unknown-world laws are deterministic and exact; active learning supports only
  one-step distinguishing continuations in resettable episodes.
- Persistent latent transfer is one two-state, six-rule, one-dimensional
  mechanism. It does not yet support stochastic effects, variable dimensions,
  multi-step experiments, or durable CAS serialization of its receipts.
- Internal mechanism mining remains contiguous-window proof-schema learning,
  not arbitrary RKL world-law induction.
- Four small synthetic families cannot establish broad general intelligence.
- ARC remains at the last measured 36/400 and is not part of this result.

## Next dependency

Generalize persistent latent schemas across varied mechanism structures and
dimensions, persist receipt lineage through CAS restart, induce competing
entities and relations from less structured observations, and search multi-step
experiments under uncertainty.
