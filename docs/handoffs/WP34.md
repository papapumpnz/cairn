## Scope delivered

- Stateful deterministic developmental controller over content-free support
  gaps, mechanism roles, prerequisites, uncertainty, transfer, cost, and risk.
- Objective-order independence and stable identity tie-breaking.
- Explicit complete, capacity, blocked-prerequisite, outstanding-work, and
  evidence-rejection terminals.
- Composition objectives become eligible only when all prerequisite mechanism
  roles are active.
- Independently verified outcomes must carry held-out transfer, strict
  memory-off candidate/sample reduction, retention, and zero negative transfer.
- One distinct verified counterexample quarantines; two deprecate. Duplicate,
  empty, and unverified evidence fails closed.
- Sustained-learning gate over repeated candidate, sample, and proof reduction,
  growing mechanism/capability counts, retention, negative-transfer rate, and
  restart evidence.
- Separated non-ARC generator and learner integration covering four different
  hidden internal causal structures at identical geometry.
- Four learned, verified, automatically promoted mechanisms reconstructed over
  two CAS restart boundaries.
- Aggregate prior-task retention 6/6 and unrelated negative transfer 0/10.
- An unseen composition objective selected after its first two prerequisites
  were learned.
- Attribution correction: repeated internal reductions are separated from the
  existing same-task G6 marginal composition curve.
- Canonical, parent-bound curriculum checkpoints at both restart boundaries.
- Fresh-controller reconstruction solely through deterministic event replay;
  no serialized mechanism status or verifier token is trusted.
- Outstanding decisions, counterexample ordering, quarantine, deprecation,
  reuse counts, and prerequisite eligibility reconstructed exactly.
- Malformed payload, missing lineage, unsupported semantics, task scope, and a
  forged recorded decision fail closed.
- Redacted failed-search summaries generate deterministic content-free
  objectives for latent context, variable cardinality, relational structure,
  composition, or value-law needs.
- Generated objective and mechanism identities cover the complete permitted
  failure structure and bound prerequisite roles.
- Dynamic objectives enter the journal at an exact event boundary; replay does
  not expose them to earlier decisions.
- Frozen G6 has no authored composition objective. A real bounded invention
  failure generates one after four mechanisms are learned, and it is selected
  after a third controller reconstruction.
- A content-free exercise request crosses to a separate proof-host materializer
  with two mandatory changed variations.
- The shared cognition runtime retrieves only persistent prerequisites and
  proposes fresh joint bindings; it cannot verify itself.
- The independent verifier reconstructs both compilations, verifies four exact
  invocations, rejects overlap/irrelevance, and issues canonical evidence.
- Generated composition completion reduces candidate work 72→46, examples
  16→2, and proof units 112→16, then survives a fourth reconstruction.

## Known limits

- Initial experiences remain generator-authored. Generated exercises are now
  materialized only at the proof-host layer.
- The materializer supplies a complete proof host; CAIRN does not yet construct
  that proof from raw observations or unknown-world interaction.
- Internal mechanism mining remains contiguous-window proof-schema learning,
  not arbitrary RKL world-law induction.
- Four small synthetic families cannot establish broad general intelligence.
- ARC remains at the last measured 36/400 and is not part of this result.

## Next dependency

let the learned mechanisms construct rather than merely match proof fragments,
and repeat the closed loop in an unknown action-conditioned world.
