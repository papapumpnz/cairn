## Interface change

```text
symbol_or_schema: relational observation/lowering/policy verification
old_contract: materializer supplied typed proof-frontier nodes and ordinal policy
new_contract: authenticated relational observation -> independent frontier
              lowering -> verified training construction -> independently bound
              structural policy -> cross-domain held-out construction
reason: cognition must derive proof evidence from generic observations and bind
        correspondence by relation structure rather than source order
downstream_packages_affected: cairn-bench, cairn-world, teacher spool, WP34 runtime
compatibility_action: existing composition APIs and persisted schemas remain
                      valid; construction APIs are additive
```

## Known limits

- Initial entity/relation evidence remains generator-authored rather than
  learned from raw sensors.
- Relational selectors are bounded two-round fingerprints, not arbitrary path
  predicates or recursive learned definitions, and remain task-local.
- Unknown-world laws are deterministic visible-state deltas; this case does not
  require latent-variable discovery or active intervention selection.
- Internal mechanism mining remains contiguous-window proof-schema learning,
  not arbitrary RKL world-law induction.
- Four small synthetic families cannot establish broad general intelligence.
- ARC remains at the last measured 36/400 and is not part of this result.
