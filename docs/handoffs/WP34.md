  gaps, mechanism roles, prerequisites, uncertainty, transfer, cost, and risk.

  empty, and unverified evidence fails closed.

  restart evidence.

- Fresh-controller reconstruction solely through deterministic event replay;
  no serialized mechanism status or verifier token is trusted.
- Outstanding decisions, counterexample ordering, quarantine, deprecation,
  reuse counts, and prerequisite eligibility reconstructed exactly.

- Materialized exercises now contain evidence-only typed frontiers, not learned

## Interface change

```text
symbol_or_schema: evidence-frontier construction proposal/policy/certificate
old_contract: materializer supplied a complete proof host for occurrence matching
new_contract: evidence-only frontier -> bounded construction -> verified
              training example -> typed policy -> held-out construction ->
              independent expected-proof verification
reason: learned mechanisms must construct future predictions, not only recognize
        supplied proof fragments
downstream_packages_affected: cairn-bench, cairn-world, teacher spool, WP34 runtime
compatibility_action: existing composition APIs and persisted schemas remain
                      valid; construction APIs are additive
```

## Known limits

- Initial experiences remain generator-authored. Generated exercises are now
  materialized as typed proof-evidence frontiers.
- CAIRN constructs the learned portion and joint result, but does not yet lower
  raw observations or unknown-world interaction into the evidence frontier.
- The learned correspondence policy uses typed ordinals, not induced relational
  selectors, and remains task-local.
- Internal mechanism mining remains contiguous-window proof-schema learning,
  not arbitrary RKL world-law induction.
- Four small synthetic families cannot establish broad general intelligence.
- ARC remains at the last measured 36/400 and is not part of this result.
